# AI-Powered Review Discovery Engine

> **Project Vision:** A scalable AI-driven research engine for Spotify that automatically synthesizes thousands of public feedback points into structured, actionable insights to inform product strategy, improve recommendations, and elevate the music discovery experience.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Develop an AI-powered Review Discovery Engine that analyzes large-scale user feedback concerning music streaming and content discovery on Spotify. The system will collect, process, and synthesize insights across multiple public channels, including:

- **App Stores:** App Store & Google Play Store reviews
- **Social & Communities:** Reddit discussions, community forums, and broad social media conversations

## ⚙️ Core Requirements

Design an AI-native workflow capable of the following end-to-end processing pipeline:

1. **Ingestion & Aggregation:** Automatically collect user feedback from disparate sources.
2. **Data Pre-processing:** Clean, deduplicate, and structure unstructured raw text.
3. **Advanced NLP:** Perform sentiment analysis, topic clustering, and theme extraction.
4. **Insight Extraction:** Identify recurring pain points, user goals, and unmet needs.
5. **Contextual Segmentation:** Filter and categorize feedback by user persona, listening behavior, and specific use cases.
6. **Evidence Generation:** Support all generated insights with direct, representative user quotes.
7. **Knowledge Base Creation:** Build an interactive, explorable interface for continuous product discovery research.

## 🔍 Key Research Questions

The engine is built to automatically extract answers to critical user experience questions:

### 1. Music Discovery Challenges
- *Friction Points:* Why do users struggle to discover new music, and what prevents them from finding content they enjoy?
- *Journey Gaps:* Which discovery journeys create the highest user friction?

### 2. Recommendation Frustrations
- *System Flaws:* What are the most common complaints about the current recommendation systems?
- *Algorithmic Fatigue:* When do recommendations feel repetitive, irrelevant, or inaccurate, and what patterns emerge across different user segments?

### 3. User Goals & Behaviors
- *Listening Outcomes:* What specific outcomes are users trying to achieve when opening the app?
- *Discovery Habits:* How do users currently discover music, and what workflows do they rely on to explore new content?

### 4. Repeat Listening Behavior
- *Exploration Barriers:* What factors discourage the exploration of new content?
- *Comfort Zones:* Why do users repeatedly listen to the same songs, playlists, or artists, and which behaviors strongly correlate with high content repetition?

### 5. User Segmentation
- *Persona Differences:* How do discovery challenges differ between casual listeners and power users?
- *Demographic Influences:* Are discovery problems influenced by age, genre preference, region, or listening frequency?

### 6. Unmet Needs & Opportunities
- *Consistent Gaps:* What unmet needs consistently surface across public reviews and discussions?
- *Feature Demands:* Which feature requests occur most frequently, and what untapped opportunities exist to boost engagement?

## 📊 Expected Output

The system must deliver a comprehensive set of structured outputs:

- **Top Recurring Themes & Emerging Trends**
- **Detailed Sentiment Breakdowns**
- **Actionable Pain Point Summaries**
- **Clustered Feature Requests**
- **Distinct User Segments**
- **Representative User Quotes**

*Ultimately, the engine will serve as a continuous pulse on the user experience, providing evidence-backed, actionable product insights at scale.*
